## What Makes a News-Style AI Voice Different

Not all AI voices suit news content. News voiceovers require specific qualities:

- **Neutral tone and pacing**: News voices avoid emotion or sing-song inflection. They're measured and authoritative.
- **Clear articulation**: Every word must be distinct. Mumbled or unclear speech destroys credibility.
- **Professional depth**: A slightly lower pitch and warm timbre build trust with listeners.
- **Consistency across clips**: Your audience recognizes and remembers your voice brand.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Creating News Voiceovers with Labs AI

### 1. Write Your News Script for AI Voiceover

AI voices read exactly what you write, so script quality determines output quality.

- Keep sentences short and punchy, 10-15 words maximum
- Use active voice: "Markets rallied today" not "A rally occurred in markets"
- Add punctuation for natural pauses: periods create short breaks, colons prepare listeners for important information
- Avoid sarcasm, irony, or complex wordplay, AI may misinterpret tone
- Include names and technical terms spelled clearly if they're uncommon

### 2. Select Your Broadcast-Style Voice

Open Labs AI on iOS and explore voices in the voice library. Test 3-5 neutral voices:

- Listen to sample clips of each voice reading news-style content
- Check for clarity at normal and fast speeds
- Note which voice feels most trustworthy for your news vertical (tech news, business, politics, health)
- Consider voice cloning if you want consistency across dozens of videos, Labs AI's voice cloning lets you clone your own voice or a voice you've licensed

### 3. Generate Your Voiceover

Once you've selected a voice:

- Paste your script into Labs AI
- Choose your language and voice
- Adjust speed if needed: news content usually works at 1.0x to 1.1x speed for clarity
- Generate and preview the audio
- Make script edits if pacing feels off, then regenerate

### 4. Download and Edit

Labs AI exports MP3 files directly. Import into your video editor:

- Align voiceover with B-roll or news footage
- Add graphics, captions, or lower-thirds
- Layer in background music at low volume, 20-30% of voiceover volume
- Export and publish

## Best Practices for News Content on Social Platforms

**For TikTok and Instagram Reels**: Keep scripts under 60 seconds. Pair voiceovers with fast-paced B-roll. News audiences on these platforms value speed and visual interest.

**For YouTube**: News voiceovers work for compilations, recaps, or analysis videos. Longer scripts (3-5 minutes) work here. Add captions since many viewers watch muted.

**For LinkedIn**: Professional news and industry updates perform well. Use a slightly more formal voice. Scripts should be 60-120 seconds for optimal engagement.

**For podcasts and newsletters**: Convert written news into audio. Voice cloning ensures your audience hears the same voice every episode, building familiarity and trust.

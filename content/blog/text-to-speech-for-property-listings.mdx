Property listing videos are no longer optional in real estate marketing, they're essential. ## Step-by-Step Guide to Adding AI Voice to Listing Videos

### Step 1: Write Your Property Description Script

Start with a compelling script highlighting the property's best features. Keep it concise, around 30-60 seconds. Include:

- Opening hook ("Discover luxury living...")
- Key property features (bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage)
- Special amenities (hardwood floors, pool, updated kitchen)
- Neighborhood highlights
- Call to action

### Step 2: Choose Your AI Voice

Download Labs AI from the App Store and browse 100+ professional voices. You can select voices by:

- Gender and age range
- Accent and language
- Tone (professional, friendly, energetic)

If you want complete brand consistency, use Labs AI's voice cloning feature to create a voice based on a sample recording. This makes every listing unmistakably yours.

### Step 3: Generate Your Narration

Paste your script into Labs AI and adjust settings:

- Speaking speed (slower for luxury properties, moderate for standard listings)
- Tone and emotion (warm for family homes, professional for commercial)
- Emphasis and pacing

Generate the audio and preview it immediately. Make adjustments if needed and regenerate in seconds.

### Step 4: Export and Edit

Download your audio file from Labs AI. Import it into your video editing software (CapCut, iMovie, Adobe Premiere, or DaVinci Resolve).

### Step 5: Sync Audio with Video Footage

Layer your AI-generated narration over property footage, drone shots, and interior walkthroughs. Keep narration timing synced with visual reveals for maximum impact.

### Step 6: Add Music and Finalizations

Include subtle background music to enhance the video without overpowering the narration. Add text overlays for key details (price, square footage, contact information).

## Real Estate AI Voice Best Practices

**Choose the right voice personality**: A warm, conversational tone works better for residential properties. Professional and authoritative voices suit luxury or commercial listings.

**Match pacing to property type**: Speak faster when describing a vibrant urban condo, slower when highlighting a serene countryside estate.

**Emphasize unique selling points**: Use natural emphasis in your script to highlight what makes this property special.

**Keep it professional**: Avoid overly robotic-sounding voices by selecting natural-sounding AI options from Labs AI's extensive library.

**Test with your audience**: Share videos with trusted colleagues and gather feedback on voice effectiveness.

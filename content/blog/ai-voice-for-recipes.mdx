## Step-by-Step Guide to Creating Recipe Video Voiceovers

### Step 1: Write Your Recipe Script

Start by writing out your recipe narration as a conversational script. Include ingredient lists, cooking instructions, and helpful tips. Keep sentences short and punchy, as AI voices perform best with natural pacing. Aim for about 100-150 words per minute of video time.

Example snippet: "Today we're making chocolate chip cookies. You'll need two cups of flour, one cup of butter, and one cup of chocolate chips. Mix your dry ingredients in a large bowl, then add the wet ingredients."

### Step 2: Choose Your AI Voice

This is where your recipe video's personality comes through. Do you want a warm, conversational tone? A energetic, enthusiastic voice? A calm, instructional style? Labs AI offers 100+ voices across 50+ languages, so you can match your brand perfectly.

For recipe content, consider voices that sound natural and approachable rather than robotic. Test 2-3 options with a short excerpt of your script to find the best fit.

### Step 3: Generate Your Voiceover

Paste your complete script into Labs AI on your iPhone. Select your chosen voice and adjust playback speed if needed. Slower speeds work well for complex recipes, while normal to fast pacing suits casual cooking content.

Generate the audio file and listen through once to check for any awkward phrasing. If something sounds off, edit your script and regenerate that section.

### Step 4: Export and Sync to Video

Download your voiceover as an MP3 file. Import it into your video editing software (DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro, or Final Cut Pro). Sync the audio with your video footage, adjusting timing so the narration matches on-screen actions.

For example, the voiceover mentioning "mix your dry ingredients" should align with footage of you mixing.

### Step 5: Add Captions and Music

While your voiceover plays, add captions for accessibility and engagement. Include trending background music at low volume to enhance the viewing experience without overwhelming the narration.

## Advanced Tips for Recipe Voiceovers

**Voice Cloning for Brand Consistency:** If you want a truly unique voice, use voice cloning to create a signature sound based on your own voice or a performer's voice. This builds stronger brand recognition across your entire recipe library.

**Multiple Language Voiceovers:** Expand your audience by generating the same recipe in Spanish, French, Arabic, Portuguese, and other languages Labs AI supports. One video, multiple markets.

**Pacing Control:** Match your voiceover speed to your video editing pace. Quick cuts with faster narration work for trendy recipe videos. Slower pacing suits detailed, educational cooking tutorials.

## Setting Up Your Fiction Voiceover Workflow

### Step 1: Prepare Your Manuscript

Start by formatting your text for AI voice. Clean up your manuscript and consider:

- Breaking longer passages into shorter segments for better pacing
- Using punctuation to guide emphasis and pauses
- Adding stage directions in brackets if you want specific emotional tones
- Identifying character dialogue that might benefit from distinct voices

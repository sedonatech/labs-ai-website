## Step-by-Step Guide to Creating Explainer Video Voiceovers

### Step 1: Write Your Explainer Script

Before touching any software, write a clear, concise script. Explainer videos work best with:

- Short sentences (10-15 words max)
- Active voice and simple vocabulary
- A conversational tone
- Pacing that matches your animation speed (roughly 150 words per minute)

Test your script by reading it aloud. If you stumble, your audience will too. Aim for 30-90 seconds for most explainer videos.

### Step 3: Input Your Script and Select Your Voice

Copy your explainer script into your chosen text-to-speech app. Browse voice options: you're looking for something that matches your brand personality. A financial explainer might use a professional, authoritative voice. A fitness app tutorial might benefit from something more energetic and encouraging.

### Step 4: Fine-Tune Pacing and Emphasis

Generated voiceovers can sound robotic if you don't adjust timing and emphasis. Most platforms let you:

- Add pauses between sentences
- Adjust speed (slower for complex concepts, faster for casual intros)
- Emphasize key words with pitch variations
- Control breathing between phrases

Listen to your output alongside your animation draft. The voiceover should breathe with your visuals, not race ahead or lag behind.

### Step 5: Export and Integrate into Your Video Editor

Once your **AI voice explainer** is perfect, export it as an MP3 or WAV file. Import the audio into your video editing software (Premiere, Final Cut, DaVinci Resolve, etc.) and sync it with your animation timeline.

## Where AI Voice Still Has Limitations

Transparency matters here. AI voices don't excel in every scenario.

AI voices struggle with heavily accented speech patterns, extremely informal or slang-heavy content, and real-time conversational back-and-forth. Poetry with unconventional punctuation can sometimes throw off intonation. And while emotion recognition has improved dramatically, subtle, nuanced emotional performances still belong to human actors.

For specialized use cases like ASMR content, certain comedy timing requirements, or deeply personal narratives, human voice remains superior.

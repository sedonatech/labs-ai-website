**TL;DR:** Use Labs AI on iOS to generate AI voiceovers from text, export the audio file, then add it to your X videos using third-party editing apps or X's native tools. Labs AI offers 100+ voices and 50+ languages, making it ideal for multilingual Twitter video content.

## Why Add AI Voice to Your X Videos?

X (formerly Twitter) videos get significantly more engagement when they include narration. Whether you're sharing breaking news, product demos, or educational content, adding a professional voiceover transforms static video posts into compelling multimedia experiences.

Here's why AI voice narration matters for X creators:

- Increased watch-through rates when videos have clear narration
- Better accessibility for audiences watching without sound
- Professional presentation without hiring voice actors
- Consistent branding across all your video content
- Ability to quickly repurpose written content into video format

## Step-by-Step Guide: Adding AI Voice to X Videos

### Step 1: Download Labs AI and Create Your Voiceover

Start by downloading **Labs AI** from the App Store. It's free and requires no sign-up for basic features. Once installed, open the app and follow these steps:

1. Tap the microphone icon or text input field
2. Paste or type the script you want narrated
3. Select your preferred voice from 100+ available options
4. Choose the language (50+ languages supported)
5. Adjust speech rate and tone if needed
6. Tap "Generate" to create your voiceover
7. Once ready, tap the share button and select "Save to Files" to download the audio file

Labs AI generates high-quality MP3 files that work perfectly for video editing.

### Step 2: Edit Your Video with the Voiceover

While X doesn't offer native voiceover tools directly in the tweet composer, you have several options:

**Option A: Use the iMovie App**

- Open iMovie and create a new project
- Import your X video clip
- Go to the audio section and tap the plus icon
- Select "Audio" and choose your Labs AI-generated voiceover
- Adjust the audio volume and timing
- Export the final video as an MP4

**Option B: Use CapCut (Recommended)**

CapCut offers more flexibility for social media creators:

- Create a new project and import your video
- Tap "Audio" at the bottom
- Select "Upload" and choose your Labs AI voiceover file
- Position the audio to align with your video content
- Adjust volume levels and add transitions as needed
- Export optimized for X's video specifications

**Option C: Upload Directly to X with Separate Audio**

Some creators upload the video and audio separately by:

1. Creating the video with visual elements
2. Using X's native tools to add subtitles or captions
3. Uploading the Labs AI audio alongside promotional tweets

### Step 3: Upload Your Video to X

Once your edited video is ready:

1. Open the X app on iPhone
2. Tap the compose button (feather icon)
3. Select "Add media" and choose your edited video file
4. Write your tweet copy
5. Add relevant hashtags like #AIVoice or #VideoMarketing
6. Tap "Post" to share with your followers

## Pro Tips for X Video Voiceovers

**Choose the Right Voice:** Labs AI's voice library includes professional, casual, and character voices. For business content, opt for clear, articulate voices. For entertainment or lifestyle content, experiment with more expressive options.

**Match Pacing to Content:** News updates benefit from faster speech rates, while educational content works better at moderate speeds. Adjust this in Labs AI before generating.

**Use Multilingual Features:** If your audience spans multiple countries, Labs AI's 50+ language support lets you create localized versions of the same video quickly. This is particularly powerful for news outlets and global brands.

**Maintain Consistency:** Consider cloning your voice (Labs AI's voice cloning feature) to maintain brand consistency across all video content. Learn more in our [voice cloning guide](voice-cloning-content-creators).
